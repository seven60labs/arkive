The 3-3-3 listening plan

Turn your radio on at 3:00, then every 3 hours, for 3 minutes, on channel 3 (FRS/GMRS).

When batteries are precious, nobody can listen all day. A shared schedule means scattered people are all listening at the same moments. A common version is:

  • When: 3:00, 6:00, 9:00 and 12:00, morning and evening (every 3 hours, starting at 3).
  • How long: 3 minutes, starting on the hour.
  • Where: FRS/GMRS channel 3, privacy codes off.
  • What: listen first; then give short check-ins and urgent needs.

This is a community convention, not a law, and different groups use different versions. Agree on it with family and neighbours before an emergency, and pick a back-up channel (for example channel 1 or 20) in case 3 is busy.

Sources (checked November 2026): 47 CFR Part 95 (FCC Personal Radio Services); Ready.gov: emergency kit.
