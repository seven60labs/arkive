CB (Citizens Band) radio

CB uses 40 AM channels around 27 MHz. No licence is needed. Range is usually a few miles; trucks and rural areas still use it.

ChannelMHzUse
927.065Emergency and traveller assistance; reserved for that by FCC rule
1927.185Highway and trucker channel: road conditions, accidents, closures

Maximum power is 4 W on AM (12 W peak on single sideband). Channel 19 is a good place to ask drivers for road information.

Sources (checked November 2026): 47 CFR Part 95 (FCC Personal Radio Services).
