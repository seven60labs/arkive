3-3-3

Radio on at 3:00, every 3 hours, for 3 minutes, on channel 3.

Message: WHO · WHERE · WHAT · NEED

Who you are · where you are · what happened · what you need. Then release and listen.

Rules of thumb

Listen first · lowest power that works · get high · keep it short · life in danger and nothing else works: any radio may be used (FCC 97.403/97.405, 95.1731).
