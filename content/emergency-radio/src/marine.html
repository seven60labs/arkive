Marine VHF

Boats use VHF channels around 156 to 157 MHz. On land, a marine radio is mainly useful for listening: using one ashore needs special authorisation.

ChannelMHzUse
16156.800Distress, safety and calling. Monitored by the Coast Guard. Call here, then move to another channel.
9156.450Boater calling channel (recreational), keeps 16 clear
22A157.100Coast Guard liaison and safety broadcasts. After a call on 16 the Coast Guard often says "switch to 22 Alpha". Radio must be set to US mode.
Distress calls: "MAYDAY" for grave and immediate danger to life or a vessel; "PAN-PAN" for urgent but not life-threatening problems; "SÉCURITÉ" for safety warnings.

Sources (checked November 2026): USCG Navigation Center: VHF marine channels.
