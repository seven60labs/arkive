MURS (Multi-Use Radio Service)

MURS is five VHF channels that anyone in the US may use without a licence, at up to 2 W. Because it is VHF, it often works better than FRS in open or wooded country, and external antennas are allowed.

Some volunteer search teams and ranches use MURS. Listen before you talk.
