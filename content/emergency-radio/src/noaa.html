NOAA Weather Radio

The National Weather Service broadcasts forecasts, watches and warnings 24 hours a day on seven VHF frequencies. Many weather radios and handhelds can receive them; you only listen, never transmit.

Common labelMHz
WX1162.400
WX2162.425
WX3162.450
WX4162.475
WX5162.500
WX6162.525
WX7162.550

Only one or two will be audible where you are; scan all seven. Radios with SAME alerting can be set to your county code so they only sound for local warnings. Channel labels (WX1 to WX7) differ between brands, so trust the frequency.

Sources (checked November 2026): NOAA Weather Radio All Hazards (weather.gov/nwr).
