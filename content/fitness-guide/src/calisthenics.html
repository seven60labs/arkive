Bodyweight strength

Do moves slowly and with control. Breathe out on the effort. B beginner I intermediate. Rest 30 to 90 seconds between sets.

Push-up progressions

Hands just wider than shoulders, body in one straight line from head to heels, lower chest toward the surface, push back up.

  1. Wall push-up (hands on a wall)
  2. Counter or table push-up
  3. Knee push-up on the floor
  4. Full push-up
  5. Slow full push-up (3 seconds down) or feet on a step

B 2 sets of 8 to 12 at the easiest level that feels challenging. I 3 sets of 10 to 20; move up a level when you can do 3 sets of 15.

Squat

Feet shoulder width, sit hips back and down as if to a chair, knees follow toes, chest up, heels down. Stand up through the whole foot.

B sit-to-stand from a chair, 2 x 10. I full squat 3 x 15 to 20, or pause 2 seconds at the bottom.

Lunge

Step one foot forward, lower until both knees bend about 90 degrees, front knee over the ankle, push back to standing. Hold a wall or chair for balance if needed.

B split squat (feet stay in place) 2 x 6 each leg. I alternating or reverse lunges 3 x 10 each leg.

Glute bridge

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat. Squeeze your buttocks and lift hips until knees, hips and shoulders line up. Pause, lower slowly.

B 2 x 12. I single-leg bridge 3 x 8 each side.

Plank

Forearms under shoulders, body straight, squeeze stomach and buttocks, do not let hips sag. Breathe normally.

B on knees or hands on a table, 3 x 15 to 20 seconds. I 3 x 30 to 60 seconds; add side planks.

Chair dips

Use a sturdy chair against a wall. Hands on the front edge, fingers forward, bend elbows straight back to about 90 degrees, push up. Keep shoulders down, away from ears. Skip if it hurts the front of your shoulder.

B knees bent, 2 x 6 to 8. I legs straight, 3 x 10 to 15.

Step-ups

Use a stable step or the bottom stair. Step up with one foot, press through that heel to stand tall, step down slowly.

B low step, 2 x 8 each leg, holding a rail. I higher step, 3 x 12 each leg.

Burpees

Squat, put hands on the floor, step or jump feet back to a plank, return feet, stand up (add a small jump if you like). This is hard on the heart; intermediate only.

B try "step-back burpees" with no jump, 2 x 5. I 3 x 8 to 12, or 20 seconds on / 40 seconds off for 5 rounds.

Making it harder over time

  1. Add reps (up to the top of the range).
  2. Add a set.
  3. Slow the lowering part to 3 seconds.
  4. Move to the next harder version and drop back to fewer reps.
