Fitness cheat sheet

Weekly target

150 min moderate activity (brisk walk) + 2 strength sessions + a few minutes of stretching and balance most days.

Every session

Warm up 5 min (march, arm circles, hip circles, half squats) · work at "talk, not sing" · stop sets with 2 to 3 reps in reserve · stretch 5 min after.

Stop now if

Chest pain or pressure, unusual breathlessness, dizziness, racing or irregular heartbeat, sharp joint pain, numbness. Chest pain that does not stop with rest: get help.

Stretch (20 to 30 s, no bouncing)

Neck · chest in doorway · cat-cow · hip flexor · hamstring · figure four · quad · calf · spinal twist.

Yoga sun salutation

Arms up · fold · half lift · plank, lower down · cobra · down dog 3 breaths · step forward, half lift · fold · rise, arms up · mountain.

Breathing

Box: in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Calm: in 4, out 6 for 3 to 5 min. Anxious: 5 see, 4 touch, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste.

Recover

Sleep 7 to 9 h · pale yellow urine · protein each meal · rest day between hard sessions · light walking when sore.
