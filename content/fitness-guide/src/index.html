Fitness Guide

A short, plain-language guide to staying strong, flexible and calm with no equipment, in a small space. It suits beginners and people coming back to exercise. Everything here works offline.

Start here: read Start safely, then try the beginner week. The one-page cheat sheet fits on a single printed page.

The short version

  • Move a little every day; something is always better than nothing.
  • Aim over a week for about 150 minutes of moderate activity (brisk walking counts) and 2 strength sessions.
  • Start easier than you think, add a little each week.
  • Good form first, then more reps, then harder versions.
  • Sleep, water and rest days are part of training.
