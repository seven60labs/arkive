Sample weekly plans

All plans need about 2 x 2 metres of floor and a sturdy chair. Always start with the 5-minute warm-up and finish with a few stretches. Moves are on the bodyweight page.

Beginner (15 to 20 minutes, weeks 1 to 4)

DaySession
MonStrength A: sit-to-stand 2x10, wall or counter push-up 2x10, glute bridge 2x12, plank 3x15 s
TueBrisk walk 15 to 20 min
WedYoga: 3 sun salutations + tree, child's pose, resting pose
ThuStrength B: split squat 2x6 each, knee push-up 2x6, step-ups 2x8 each, side plank 2x10 s each
FriBrisk walk 15 to 20 min
SatStretching routine + box breathing 3 min
SunRest or easy walk

Each week add 2 reps or 5 seconds, or 5 minutes of walking. After 4 easy-feeling weeks, try intermediate.

Intermediate (25 to 30 minutes)

DaySession
MonStrength A: squat 3x15, push-up 3x10 to 15, single-leg bridge 3x8 each, plank 3x45 s
TueIntervals: 5 rounds of 20 s burpees / 40 s marching, then 15 min brisk walk
WedYoga 20 min: 5 sun salutations, warrior II, tree, cobra, bridge, resting pose
ThuStrength B: reverse lunge 3x10 each, chair dips 3x10, step-ups 3x12 each, side plank 3x30 s each
FriCircuit: 3 rounds of 10 squats, 10 push-ups, 10 lunges, 30 s plank, rest 1 min
SatLong walk 30 to 60 min + stretching
SunRest, body scan

Short on time? (10 minutes)

Warm-up 2 min, then 2 rounds: 10 squats, 8 push-ups (any level), 10 bridges, 20 s plank. Finish with 1 minute slow breathing.
