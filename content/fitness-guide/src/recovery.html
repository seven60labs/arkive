Recovery, sleep and water

Rest days

Muscles get stronger between sessions. Leave at least one day between hard strength sessions for the same muscles. Light walking and stretching on rest days helps soreness.

Soreness

Stiffness 1 to 2 days after a new workout is normal and fades. Gentle movement helps more than complete rest. Very severe pain, swelling, or dark (cola-coloured) urine after hard exercise needs medical attention.

Sleep

  • Adults need about 7 to 9 hours; teens 8 to 10.
  • Keep a regular wake time, even on weekends.
  • Get daylight in the morning; dim lights and screens in the last hour before bed.
  • Avoid caffeine late in the day and heavy meals right before sleep.
  • Cool, dark, quiet room. If you cannot sleep after about 20 minutes, get up and do something calm, then try again.

Water

  • Drink regularly through the day; pale yellow urine is a good sign.
  • Drink more in heat, at altitude, when sweating, or when ill.
  • For long sweaty sessions (over an hour), salt and sugar help: food plus water, or an oral rehydration drink.
  • Do not force huge amounts; too much water too fast can also be dangerous.

Food basics

  • Eat some protein at each meal (beans, lentils, eggs, fish, meat, dairy, nuts).
  • Plenty of vegetables, fruit and whole grains.
  • A small snack 1 to 2 hours before training if you are hungry.

Heat and cold

In hot weather exercise early or late, go easier, and stop for headache, nausea, confusion or no longer sweating (heat illness). In cold, dress in layers and warm up longer.
