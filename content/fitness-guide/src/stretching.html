Stretching and mobility

Stretch when you are warm, for example after a walk or workout. Ease into a mild pull, never pain. Breathe slowly; hold each stretch 20 to 30 seconds, both sides. Do not bounce.

10-minute daily routine

  1. Neck: tilt ear toward shoulder, then chin toward chest.
  2. Chest: forearm on a doorframe, gently turn body away.
  3. Upper back: clasp hands in front, round your back and reach forward.
  4. Cat and cow: on hands and knees, slowly round then arch your back, 8 times.
  5. Hip flexor: half-kneel, tuck pelvis and shift gently forward.
  6. Hamstring: sit, one leg straight, hinge forward from the hips with a long back.
  7. Glute (figure four): on your back, ankle over the opposite knee, pull the legs toward you.
  8. Quad: standing, hold a wall, bring heel toward buttock, knees together.
  9. Calf: hands on a wall, back leg straight, heel down.
  10. Spinal twist: lying down, knees fall to one side, look the other way.

Desk or long-sitting break (3 minutes, every hour)

  • Stand up and march 30 seconds
  • 10 shoulder rolls back
  • Chin tucks: slide chin straight back, 10 times
  • Standing back bend with hands on hips, 5 gentle times
  • 10 calf raises
  • Look at something far away for 20 seconds

Mobility for joints

Slow circles keep joints moving: ankles, knees (gently), hips, wrists, shoulders, 5 each way. Good first thing in the morning.
