Beginner yoga

Yoga here is simple stretching and balance practice with steady breathing. Use a towel or blanket on the floor. Move with the breath, never force a pose, and skip anything that hurts. Hold each pose for 3 to 5 slow breaths.

Ten basic poses

1. Mountain

Stand tall, feet hip width, weight even, arms by your sides, crown of the head lifting. The starting point for standing poses.

2. Forward fold

From standing, hinge at the hips and let your upper body hang. Bend your knees as much as you need. Roll up slowly.

3. Downward-facing dog

From hands and knees, tuck toes and lift hips up and back into an upside-down V. Knees can stay bent; heels do not need to touch the floor.

4. Cat and cow

On hands and knees, breathe in and let the belly drop with the gaze forward (cow); breathe out and round the back (cat).

5. Child's pose

Kneel, sit back toward your heels, fold forward and rest your forehead on the floor or a cushion, arms forward or by your sides. A resting pose any time.

6. Cobra

Lie face down, hands under shoulders. Gently lift your chest using your back muscles, elbows bent and close in, shoulders down.

7. Warrior II

Step feet wide, turn the front foot out, bend the front knee over the ankle, arms out level with the floor, look over the front hand.

8. Tree

Stand on one leg, place the other foot on your ankle, calf or inner thigh (never on the knee). Hands at the chest or overhead. Hold a wall if needed.

9. Bridge

Same as the glute bridge: lie on your back, knees bent, lift your hips and breathe steadily.

10. Resting pose

Lie on your back, arms and legs relaxed, eyes closed, for 2 to 5 minutes. Let the breath be natural.

Sun salutation (simple version)

Move one step per breath. Repeat 3 to 5 rounds, slowly.

  1. Mountain, breathe in and raise arms overhead.
  2. Breathe out, forward fold (knees bent if needed).
  3. Breathe in, half lift: hands on shins, back flat.
  4. Breathe out, step back to a plank, then lower knees, chest and chin (or lie down).
  5. Breathe in, cobra.
  6. Breathe out, downward dog; stay for 3 breaths.
  7. Breathe in, step feet to hands, half lift.
  8. Breathe out, forward fold.
  9. Breathe in, roll up to stand with arms overhead; breathe out, hands down to mountain.
Pregnancy, high blood pressure, glaucoma, or neck and back problems: skip upside-down poses like downward dog and deep back bends unless a professional says they are fine.
